===== SOLUTION SECTION 02 =====
YOU WILL START OUT IN CHIBA CITY,
THE YEAR IS 2058.  YOU ARE NEAR TOKYO
BAY IN THE KANTO DISTRICT OF SOUTH-
CENTRAL HONSHU, JAPAN. CHIBA IS A MAGNET
FOR THE SPRAWL'S TECHNO-CRIMINAL SUB-
CULTURES, NOTORIOUS FOR ITS SHADOWLAND
OF BLACK MEDICINE AND BODY SHOPS.
CRIMINAL ACTIVITY IN CHIBA IS A CONSTANT
SUBLIMINAL HUM, AND DEATH IS THE PUNISH-
MENT FOR LAZINESS, CARELESSNESS, LACK
OF GRACE, OR THE FAILURE TO HEED THE
DEMANDS OF THE INTRICATE PROTOCOL. AT
ONE END OF THE CITY IS THE TOWERING
HOLOGRAM LOGO OF FUJI ELECTRIC,
DOMINATING THE SKYLINE OVER THE HIGH-
TECH ZONE. AT THE OTHER END IS THE CHIBA
SPACEPORT, GATEWAY TO THE ORIBITAL
COLONIES OF FREESIDE AND ZION CLUSTER.

YOU ARE IN THE CHATSUBO BAR, OWNED BY
RATZ.  YOU OWE HIM 46 CREDITS FOR
THE FOOD YOU SLEPT IN.  YOU WILL
NEED TO LOG INTO THE PAX (PUBLIC ACCESS
SYSTEM) AND DOWNLOAD ANOTHER 40
CREDITS TO ADD TO THE 6 YOU HAVE
WHEN YOU START.  IF YOU CHECK THE
MESSAGES, YOU WILL FIND OUT (EVENTUALLY)
MATT SHAW HAS SENT YOU SOME LINK CODES:
CHEAPO    = CHEAP HOTEL
REGFELLOW = REGULAR FELLOWS
ASANOCOMP = ASANO COMPUTING
YOU WILL ALSO SEE A MESSAGE FROM
GENERAL ARMITAGE, ASKING EVERYBODY
TO SEND HIM YOUR BAMA ID NUMBER.
SEND HIM A MESSAGE (ARMITAGE)
WITH YOUR BAMA ID ACCOUNT NUMBER,
AND HE WILL DEPOSIT 10,000 CREDITS
INTO YOUR ACCOUNNT.

ONCE YOU ARE DONE, LOGOFF AND GIVE
46 CREDITS TO RATZ.  ONCE YOU LEAVE,
THE CHATSUBO WILL BE CLOSED FOR THE
WHOLE GAME.



YOU CAN SELL BODY PARTS TO RAISE
EXTRA CASH, BUT REMEMBER THAT IT
WILL LOWER YOUR CONSTITUTION. ALTHOUGH
THAT MAY NOT MUCH MATTER IN THE
REAL WORLD, IT WILL HAVE A VERY
LARGE IMPACT IN THE WORLD OF CYBERSPACE.
YOU WILL NOTICE THAT YOU HAVE
A HARD TIME TALKING TO THE COP AT
DONUT WORLD.  WHEN YOU ENTER
LARRY'S MICROSOFTS, ASK HIM ABOUT
CHIPS, AND HE WILL SELL YOU A COPTALK
CHIP SO YOU CAN ASK THE COP QUESTIONS.

THERE IS A MESSAGE AT THE PAX SAYING
FOR YOU TO PICK UP YOUR DECK. YOU
WILL WANT TO GO BUT SHIN'S PAWNSHOP,
LOCATED DIRECTLY SOUTH OF THE
MASSAGE PARLOR. IF YOU DO NOT HAVE
ANY MONEY, YOU CAN GET YOUR UXB DECK
FOR FREE. IT WILL HAVE COMLINK 1
ALREADY INSTALLED, SO YOU ARE READY
FOR THE DATABASES (DB).

REMEMBER THAT YOUR UXB IS NOT CYBERSPACE
COMPATIBLE, AND THAT CRAZY EDO DOES
NOT SELL CYBERSPACE COMPATIBLE DECKS,
BUT ASANO'S DOES. IF YOU INSULT
EDO, YOU CAN GET A LARGE DISCOUNT
ON CYBERSPACE DECKS.

YOU WILL FIND THE HOUSE OF PONG,
AND WILL BE SENT ON A 'HOLY MISSION'
TO FIND A JOYSTICK.  ASK FINN
AT METRO HOLOGRAFIX ABOUT 'JOYSTICK'
AND HE WILL SELL YOU ONE.

YOUR BILL AT THE CHEAP HOTEL WILL NEED
TO BE PAID, SO YOU CAN LOGIN WITH
THE LINK CODE I GAVE YOU, AND USE
THE PASSWORD COCKROACH TO EDIT
YOU BILL, AND ORDER CAVIAR TO GIVE
TO CRAZY EDO. THAT IS HOW TO GET COMLINK
2.

BELOW I WILL GIVE YOU THE INSTRUCTIONS
ON WHERE TO FIND EACH VERSION OF
THE COMLINK SOFTWARE:


COMLINK 1: INSTALLED IN YOUR UXB DECK.

COMLINK 2: BUY CAVIAR FROM CHEAP HOTEL
ROOM SERVICE AND TRADE WITH CRAZY
EDO.

COMLINK 3: VISIT THE MASSAGE PARLOR AND
ASK TO BUY SOME INFORMATION. THE FIRST
CLUE WILL GIVE THE LINK CODE AND
PASSWORD TO THE PANTHER MODERNS DB,
WHERE IT CAN BE DOWNLOADED.

COMLINK 4: AT THE SEA. ASK MODERN BOB
ON THE PANTHER MODERNS DB FOR THE LINK
CODES, AND THEN USE COPTALK TO TALK
AT THE COP AT DONUT WORLD FOR THE
ENCODED PASSWORD. USE CRYPTOLOGY LVL 2
TO DECODE SMEEGLDIPO

COMLINK 5: USING COPTALK LVL 2, YOU
LEARN THAT SHIVA KNOWS ABOUT THE LOSER
DB. ASK SHIVA ABOUT LOSER. SHE WILL
GIVE YOU THE LINK CODE AND THE PASSWORD
FOR THE LOSER DB WHERE YOU WILL FIND
THE LINK CODE FOR THE EASTERN SEABORD
FISSION AUTHORTY (ESFA). ASK
EMPEROR NORTON AT THE MATRIX RESTAURANT
ABOUT PASSWORD FOR THE ESFA PASSWORD.
COMLINK 5 CAN BE DOWNLOADED THERE.

COMLINK 6: READ THE FIRST MSG FROM THE
PRESIDENT OF HOSAKA. IT TELLS ABOUT
A JAPANESE COMPANY NAMED TOZOKU WHICH IS
RUNNING THEM OUT OF BUSINESS. SINCE
JULIUS DEANE KNOWS ABOUT JAPANESE
COMPANIES, ASK HIM ABOUT TOZOKU. HE
GIVES YOU THE LINK CODE SO YOU WILL
NEED SEQUENCER 1 FOR THE PASSWORD.
COMLINK 6 IS AT TOZOKU.

===== SOLUTION SECTION 08 =====
ONE INPORTANT EQUATION YOU MAY WANT
TO KNOW IS ABOUT THE ICE BREAKING
PRGS.
-----------------------------------
THE RANKING OF WAREZ IS AS FOLLOWS:

LEVEL 1: BLOWTORCH, DECODER, HAMMER

LEVEL 2: DRILL, DOORSTOP

LEVEL 3: DEPTCHARGE, LOGIC BOMB,
         CONCRETE
-----------------------------------
THE HIGHER THE LEVEL, THE BETTER IT
IS AT FIGHTING ICE.
EXAMPLE:
USING THIS CHART, DRILL 2 (2 X LVL 2=4)
HAS THE SAME IMPACT AS HAMMER 4
(4 X LVL 1=4).  OBVIOUSLY, A PROGRAM
SUCH AS DEPTHCHARGE 8 CARRIES A LOT
OF PUNCH. ALSO, EACH TIME YOU USE
A WARE, IT EFFECTIVENESS GOES DOWN
ONE VERSION, SO IF YOU USE HAMMER 4,
THE NEXT TIME YOU USE IT ON THE SAME
ICE, IT WILL ONLY HAVE THE EFFECT OF
OF HAMMER 3. KEEP THIS IN MIND, IT WILL
MAKE THE GAME EASIER...
